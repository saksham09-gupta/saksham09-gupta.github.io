Common questions

Does solar work on cloudy days?

Yes, at lower output. Over a full year the system is sized so the total still covers the share of your bill we quote.

What happens at night?

You use grid power as before. With net metering, extra power you send out by day is credited against what you draw at night.

How much maintenance is needed?

Mostly cleaning. We recommend every one to two months in dusty areas, and our care plan covers it.

Can I pay in instalments?

Yes. We work with several lenders, and you can ask for a loan option in your quote.
